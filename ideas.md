# Soori Post Studio — Reference-Grounded Design Spec

## Chosen Approach: Editorial Control Room

The supplied screenshots are the ground-truth visual specification: a compact, almost black Facebook-post creation workspace with a left-hand stack of numbered accordion steps and a large, sticky live preview on the right. The implementation will preserve the reference’s workflow-first feel while adapting the branding and language controls for Sinhala and English social content.

### Design Movement

Dark editorial production software with newspaper-inspired hierarchy: stark contrast, explicit labels, numbered modules, and an energetic yellow signal color.

### Core Principles

1. **Production before decoration.** Each control has a clear purpose and a visible outcome in the preview.
2. **Editorial hierarchy.** Strong type, compact metadata, and section numbering create an orderly publishing workflow.
3. **Focused contrast.** Near-black surfaces hold quiet controls back so the amber action state and the composed post can lead the eye.
4. **Bilingual fluency.** Sinhala and English are presented with consistent typographic support and no secondary-language treatment.

### Color Philosophy

The canvas is blue-black and charcoal rather than pure black, preventing eye strain while retaining the reference’s concentrated studio atmosphere. Signal yellow is intentionally reserved for actionable choices, completed stages, and reaction details. A deep newspaper navy anchors the final post artwork, while white carries headline authority.

### Layout Paradigm

A two-track desktop studio: a flexible production rail on the left and a narrower preview column pinned on the right. On smaller screens, the preview becomes a full-width composition plate above the controls so the outcome remains legible without a competing sidebar.

### Signature Elements

1. Square, numbered step markers that shift from graphite to yellow when active or complete.
2. A quiet dot-grid/print texture around preview and upload areas.
3. Editorial reaction bars with red and blue response tokens in the post artwork.

### Interaction Philosophy

Controls are direct, explain themselves through microcopy, and update the artwork immediately. Accordions maintain focus by showing the active stage in full and compressing earlier work into a descriptive summary.

### Animation

Accordions enter and exit through short opacity and vertical transform transitions with a crisp editorial cadence. Selected cards use a 160 ms border and surface transition; preview changes use a restrained dissolve. All non-essential motion respects reduced-motion preferences.

### Typography System

**Oswald** supplies condensed editorial headlines and high-impact preview copy. **Noto Sans Sinhala** supports Sinhala headlines and control language reliably. **DM Sans** maintains compact, highly legible UI metadata and input text. Headline size is assertive but adapts based on copy length.

### Brand Essence

**A focused, bilingual post studio for Sri Lankan creators who need publish-ready Facebook artwork without a heavyweight design tool.**

Personality: **editorial, direct, culturally fluent.**

### Brand Voice

Headlines are concrete, urgent, and unembellished. CTAs read like clear next actions from a creative desk, not generic invitations.

Example lines:

> Turn the story into a post people stop for.

> Choose a frame. Set the angle. Publish with intent.

### Wordmark & Logo

The SOORI mark is a compact gold sunburst breaking through a stacked slate page, signalling a bright editorial point of view. The wordmark uses condensed uppercase lettering with a small “POST STUDIO” descriptor.

### Signature Brand Color

**Signal Saffron — #F6C400**

## Style Decisions

The implementation will reproduce the screenshot’s deep-dark workspace, stepped accordion workflow, generous fixed preview column, yellow action hierarchy, compact specification text, outlined field controls, and ready-to-export social post preview without copying the source branding or content.
